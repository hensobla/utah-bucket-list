# Hero image prompt

Generate this with your AI image tool of choice (Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly,
Ideogram, etc.), save the result as **`assets/hero.jpg`** in this repo, and
it'll appear automatically in the photo band below the headline — no code
changes needed. Until you drop a file in, that band shows a steel-blue-to-ink
gradient instead of a broken image, so the site never looks unfinished.

## Primary prompt

> Wide-format editorial landscape photograph of the Utah high desert at
> golden hour: layered red-rock mesas and sandstone cliffs in the
> foreground, distant snow-dusted mountain peaks on the horizon, a wide
> open pale sky with soft directional light from a low sun. Warm amber and
> terracotta tones in the rock, grading into cool steel-blue shadows and a
> pale sky — cinematic, slightly desaturated, teal-and-orange color grade.
> Generous open negative space in the upper third of the frame. Shot on a
> full-frame camera with a wide-angle lens, sharp focus throughout, no
> people, no text, no logos, no watermark. Photorealistic, National
> Geographic travel-photography quality — clean and sophisticated, not
> stylized or illustrated.

## Specs

- **Aspect ratio:** 16:9 or wider (21:9 also works well)
- **Resolution:** at least 2400×1350px so it stays sharp on retina displays
- **Composition:** keep the main rock formation centered/lower-third — the
  band crops to roughly 200–380px tall and gets narrower on phones, so
  anything near the left/right edges may get trimmed
- **Negative prompt (if your tool supports one):** people, text, watermark,
  logo, illustration, cartoon, oversaturated, HDR halo, lens flare
