A Few Notes

  • Great Salt Lake levels remain historically low — Antelope Island's main boat ramp is closed and the Spiral Jetty shoreline has shifted with it. Worth checking current conditions if you go back.
  • Elevation-dependent spots (Cedar Breaks, Mirror Lake Highway) stay snowed in through spring — the timeline above assumes a normal snowpack, so check road status for early-season trips.
  • Snowfall varies a lot year to year (Alta swung from a record 903″ season in 2022–23 to its least-snowy season on record in 2025–26) — check current conditions rather than planning strictly off averages.
