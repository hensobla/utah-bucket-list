# Utah Bucket List: What's Left (Updated)

Here's what's done, and everything else sequenced across a roughly ten-month window, September 2026 through June 2027.

## Already Done ✓
- Snowboarding: Park City, Snowbird, Solitude, Brighton — plus skiing at Deer Valley
- Moab / Arches
- Bryce Canyon
- Lake Blanche *(you'd repeat this)*
- Antelope Island
- Fifth Water / Diamond Fork Hot Springs
- Sundance Film Festival — good timing, since 2026 was its final year in Utah before the festival relocates to Boulder, Colorado
- Alpine Loop in fall *(you'd repeat this)*
- Bear Lake weekend

*Note: I'm assuming "Moab/Arches" means Arches specifically. If you also hit Canyonlands or Dead Horse Point while there, skip that item below.*

## Do This Now — Time-Sensitive
- **Salt Lake Temple public open house reservations open September 1, 2026 at 11:00 a.m. MDT** via TempleSquare.org / the Temple Square app. The open house itself runs April 5–October 1, 2027 — the Church expects 3–5 million visitors, so free tickets (up to 6 per guest) will move fast. This is genuinely rare; the temple has been closed for a seismic retrofit and this is a limited public window before it's rededicated.
- **Utah State Fair** and the **Greek Festival** (Utah's largest ethnic festival) both hit Salt Lake in September.
- **Bonneville Speed Week** runs in August each year — if you missed 2026, note early August 2027.

## Fall 2026 (now–November)
- **Zion National Park** (~4.5 hrs) — non-technical options: Canyon Overlook Trail, the paved Riverside Walk, Emerald Pools, Watchman Trail. Cooler fall temps are ideal now.
- **Capitol Reef National Park** (~3.5 hrs, near Torrey) — the most central, least crowded Mighty 5 park. Fruita orchards (fall harvest), Hickman Bridge, Cohab Canyon; also one of Utah's best fall-color parks.
- **Canyonlands (Island in the Sky) + Dead Horse Point** (~4 hrs, near Moab) — easy add-on to a return Moab trip if your Arches visit didn't cover these: Mesa Arch, Grand View Point, and one of the Southwest's most dramatic overlooks.
- **More fall foliage drives**, beyond the Alpine Loop you already love: Big Cottonwood Canyon + Guardsman Pass, Nebo Loop (Mount Nebo Scenic Byway), Mirror Lake Highway, Logan Canyon (~1.5 hrs). Peak color is roughly late September–early October.
- **Goblin Valley + Little Wild Horse Canyon** (~3.5 hrs) — freely wander mushroom-shaped hoodoos; a non-technical narrows walk; also a certified Dark Sky park worth a night under the stars.

## Winter 2026–27 (Dec–Mar)
You've hit the marquee resorts. A few things that haven't come up yet:
- **Ski Alta** — since you ski at Deer Valley, Alta's skier-only (no snowboards) terrain is fair game; it averages 548 inches of snow a year, among the most of any US resort.
- **Snowmobiling in the Uintas** — Mirror Lake / Mill Hollow / Strawberry complex (~1 hr), 250+ miles of groomed trail. This covers the one activity on your interest list that hasn't shown up yet.
- **Homestead Crater** (Midway, ~45 min–1 hr) — swim in a 90–96°F geothermal spring inside a limestone dome; reservations required.
- **Mystic Hot Springs** (Monroe, ~2.5 hrs) — vintage clawfoot tubs, a different vibe from Fifth Water.
- **Temple Square Christmas lights** (late Nov–Jan 1) and the Tabernacle Choir's free Christmas concert.
- Consider a **new resort for variety**: Powder Mountain or Snowbasin (~1–1.5 hrs), both less crowded than the Cottonwoods.

## Spring 2027 (Apr–May)
- **Cedar Breaks National Monument** opens ~late May — a Bryce-like high-elevation amphitheater with a certified Dark Sky.
- **Grand Staircase-Escalante** — the Lower Calf Creek Falls hike (~6 mi round trip to a 130-ft waterfall and swimming hole) along Scenic Byway 12.
- **Kodachrome Basin State Park** — easy trails among colorful sandstone spires.
- Cottonwood Canyon hikes as snow melts: **Red Pine Lake**, **White Pine Lake**, **Donut Falls**, **Silver Lake at Brighton**.
- If you got Temple open house tickets, this is prime season to use them.

## Summer 2027 (Jun–Aug)
- **Cecret Lake / Albion Basin** — peak wildflowers mid-July–early August, an easy 1.7-mile hike.
- **Mount Timpanogos summit** (~14+ mi, strenuous but non-technical) — waterfalls, wildflowers, mountain goats; needs a timed-entry permit on weekends July–October.
- **Millcreek Canyon** hikes — Grandeur Peak, Dog Lake; closer to town, dog-friendly on the right days.
- **Snow Canyon State Park** (near St. George) if you swing south again.
- **Bonneville Salt Flats** — drive on the salt in dry conditions; pair with **Spiral Jetty** and the **Sun Tunnels** land-art installations out in the west desert for a full "weird Utah" day.

## Anytime / Local Culture
No seasonal urgency here — just things that haven't come up yet:
- **Temple Square beyond the temple itself**: the new Visitors' Center (opened May 2026, with a free "Inside a Temple" replica tour), Tabernacle organ recitals, Church History Museum.
- **SLC food scene**: Log Haven (Millcreek Canyon), Takashi, Red Iguana, Pretty Bird hot chicken, HSL, The Copper Onion.
- **Brewery crawl**: Fisher Brewing, Squatters, Red Rock, Epic, Level Crossing, SaltFire, Bewilder.
- **Moab scenic drives beyond Arches**: Potash Road (Scenic Byway 279), Scenic Byway 128 along the Colorado River (arguably the best river drive in the state), La Sal Mountain Loop, and a guided non-technical Jeep tour of the Shafer Trail.
- **Antelope Island Bison Roundup** — late October, a specific event distinct from a regular island visit, in case you haven't caught it.
- **Natural Bridges**, **Dinosaur National Monument**, **Timpanogos Cave** — further-flung monuments not yet mentioned.
- **Hidden gems**: Fantasy Canyon and Moonshine Arch (near Vernal), Meadow Hot Springs, Devil's Slide, the Bingham Canyon Mine overlook, Valley of the Gods / Moki Dugway in the far southeast, the quirky small town of Helper.
- **Dark-sky stargazing** beyond what you've done: Natural Bridges, Dead Horse Point, Cedar Breaks, or closer-in North Fork Park near Ogden.

## A Few Notes
- Great Salt Lake levels remain historically low — Antelope Island's main boat ramp is closed and the Spiral Jetty shoreline has shifted with it; worth checking current conditions if you go back.
- Elevation-dependent spots (Cedar Breaks, Mirror Lake Highway) stay snowed in through spring — the timing above assumes a normal snowpack, so check road status for early-season trips.
- Snowfall varies a lot year to year (Alta swung from a record 903" season in 2022–23 to its least-snowy season on record in 2025–26) — check current conditions rather than planning strictly off averages.
